Join Us

Opportunities to join or collaborate with the LIMO Lab.

LIMO welcomes inquiries from prospective PhD students and postdoctoral researchers who are interested in the mathematical foundations and practical applications of imaging, optimization, inverse problems, and machine learning.

Opportunities

PhD students

We welcome applicants with strong preparation in applied mathematics, optimization, scientific computing, statistics, machine learning, or a related field.

Opportunities

Postdoctoral researchers

Researchers working in mathematical imaging, inverse problems, optimization, or theoretically grounded machine learning are encouraged to get in touch.

How to inquire

Please send a concise email to xqzhang at sjtu dot edu dot cn with:

  1. a current curriculum vitae;
  2. a short description of your research interests and how they connect to LIMO;
  3. representative publications, preprints, code, or other relevant work; and
  4. your intended start date or application timeline.

Positions and funding depend on current availability. An inquiry is not an application or an offer; shortlisted candidates will receive information about the appropriate Shanghai Jiao Tong University application process.

Collaboration and visits

We also welcome research discussions and potential collaborations with academic, clinical, and industrial partners. In your message, please describe the problem, relevant background, and the type of collaboration you have in mind.
